The purity traps

13 questions where the answer libertarians are 'supposed' to give and the answer normal people give are very far apart.

Taxation is theft.

Purity answer: Agree

Pass: Correct, according to every libertarian T-shirt ever printed.

Fail: You don't think taxes are theft. Thousands of accounts with a yellow snake flag in their profile just unfollowed you.

The government should require a license to drive a car.

Purity answer: Disagree

Pass: No license required. The 2016 convention floor would carry you out on its shoulders.

Fail: You want driver's licenses. Somewhere, a delegate is booing and doesn't know why.

The government should limit who can buy violent video games like Grand Theft Auto.

Purity answer: Disagree

Pass: Correct. If games caused crime, every Grand Theft Auto player would be in prison.

Fail: You'd regulate a game about breaking the law. The DMV is proud of you.

Adults should be able to buy any drug, even heroin, the way they buy whiskey.

Purity answer: Agree

Pass: Even heroin. The purity committee nods solemnly.

Fail: You legalized the drugs you like and kept the rest illegal. A bold and very common strategy.

Peaceful adults should be able to own any weapon the military uses, even a tank.

Purity answer: Agree

Pass: Tank owners for liberty. Your HOA will be thrilled.

Fail: You'd let the government keep the tanks and not you. Bold trust in the people with the tanks.

We should get rid of laws that make adults wear seatbelts.

Purity answer: Agree

Pass: Your skull, your choice. Your insurance company would like a word.

Fail: You're fine with a ticket for not protecting yourself from yourself. Click it or ticket, comrade.

Roads should be privately owned and run.

Purity answer: Agree

Pass: Who will build the roads? You will, after you win the bid.

Fail: You need the state for roads. Fair. Have you seen the potholes it builds?

We'd be better off with no government at all.

Purity answer: Agree

Pass: No government at all. That's full anarcho-capitalist, and your HOA is terrified.

Fail: You want at least some government. The anarchists will call you a statist at the afterparty.

Libertarians should skip voting, because voting props up the government.

Purity answer: Agree

Pass: Purity achieved. Ballot-access petitions still need signatures, so maybe sign one anyway.

Fail: You vote. Somewhere, a guy who only pays in cash is disappointed in you.

Libertarian candidates should wear a suit, talk about potholes and try to win.

Purity answer: Disagree

Pass: Correct purity answer. Also why the party owns more T-shirts than city council seats.

Fail: A pragmatist. The purists are drafting a resolution about you right now.

A king who owns the whole country would govern better than a democracy.

Purity answer: Disagree

Pass: No kings. The Gadsden flag approves.

Fail: Bold choice for a movement whose favorite flag was made to fight a king.

Liberty only works for people who share my culture and heritage.

Purity answer: Disagree

Pass: Correct. Liberty doesn't come with a membership card.

Fail: Ayn Rand called racism “the lowest, most crudely primitive form of collectivism.” She was talking to you.

Private citizens should be able to own nuclear weapons.

Purity answer: Agree

Pass: You'd allow private nukes. You are libertarian enough. Please never run for office.

Fail: No private nukes? Statist. (Kidding. Mostly.)

Why we roast purity tests while giving you one

Because the purity test is the movement's favorite hobby and its worst habit. A movement that boos driver's licenses on live TV is not a movement that's going to win your county commission. Pass every trap and you're libertarian enough. Then read the organizing playbook and figure out what to do with it.

The driver's license moment is real: at the Libertarian Party's 2016 presidential debate, a candidate who said he supported driver's licenses got booed. The story is on the controversies page.

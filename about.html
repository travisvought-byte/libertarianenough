About Libertarian Enough

What this is

Libertarian Enough is an independent libertarian organizing project. It is not a political party, has no ballot line, takes no donations, and is not affiliated with the Libertarian National Committee or any state party. It's a cheeky quiz on top of a serious toolkit: a guide to the movement for people outside it, and organizing and fundraising playbooks for people inside it.

It's made in Ohio by Travis Vought, a libertarian organizer and the author of The Asymmetric Libertarian. Every jab on this site is aimed from inside the tent.

How the quiz scores you

There are 40 statements. Each answer counts from -2 (strongly disagree) to +2 (strongly agree). Each statement feeds one or more of these scores, some in reverse:

  • Economic freedom: taxes, minimum wage, licensing, tariffs, bailouts, the Fed, school money, raw milk.
  • Personal freedom: drugs, sex work, speech, guns, marriage, immigration, warrants, seatbelts, abortion.
  • Radical: anarchy, private roads, abolishing patents, secession, private nukes.
  • Left-libertarian: co-ops, unions, landlords, corporate cronyism.
  • Traditional: families and churches, secession, kings, heritage, and tariffs and immigration in reverse.
  • Pragmatism: wearing a suit and trying to win.
  • Constitutional: the Constitution as written, warrants, licensing.
  • Mises Caucus energy: the Fed, the wars, politeness, lockdowns.
  • Voluntaryist: not voting, crypto and the gray market.

Each score is scaled from -1 to +1. Your spot on the chart is economic freedom and personal freedom, each turned into 0 to 100.

Purity score = 40% economic freedom + 40% personal freedom + 20% radical. If you say liberty only works for people who share your heritage, it's zero, and you're a Collectivist in a Gadsden Flag.

Your type: within 10 points of the middle on both axes, you're a centrist. Top right with both scores 60 or more, the quiz picks the libertarian flavor your other scores point to strongest: anarcho-capitalist, agorist, left-libertarian, paleo, Mises Caucus energy, classical liberal, pragmatist, purist (purity 90 or higher, with little pragmatism) or minarchist. If none of those pulls strongly, or you're top right but under 60 on either axis, you're a small-l libertarian. The other corners are progressive, conservative and statist.

The famous people and archetypes on the chart are our estimates, for fun. They didn't take the quiz.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • Your quiz answers stay in your browser. They're saved there so you can come back, and they're written into your results link after the # sign. Browsers don't send that part of a link to any server, so we never see your answers unless you send someone your link.
  • Visitor counts: we use GoatCounter, which counts page views without cookies and doesn't store your IP address. It sees which page you opened, never your answers.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Sources and data

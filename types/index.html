Libertarian Enough / Types

All 15 types

Nine kinds of libertarian, four kinds of not, one centrist and one collectivist hiding in a Gadsden flag.

Minarchist

Government should be small enough to drown, but not dead.

You want a night-watchman state: police, courts, defense, and not one agency more.

Anarcho-Capitalist

Who will build the roads? You will, after you win the bid.

You think every job the state does, a market could do better, including the courts.

Agorist

Why vote when you can just not file?

You'd rather build a parallel economy than win an election. Voluntary everything.

Left-Libertarian

Abolish the state and the landlord.

You're anti-state and anti-boss, and you see corporate power riding on government power.

Paleolibertarian

Small government, big fences.

You want the state out of your wallet but you lean traditional and wary of open borders.

Mises Caucus Energy

End the Fed. Also, end the thread.

You want the movement louder, harder-edged and focused on the Fed, war and lockdowns.

Classical Liberal

Adam Smith, but make it a TED Talk.

Free markets, free speech, rule of law. You think the Enlightenment mostly nailed it.

Pragmatist

You think wearing a suit is a valid campaign strategy.

You want libertarians to win offices, fix potholes and stop fighting about nukes.

Purist

You'd rather lose 99 to 1 than win with a driver's license.

Principle first, second and third. If a candidate compromises, you're out.

Small-l Libertarian

Libertarian, but you'd never put it in your bio.

You lean free on money and on life. You don't need the label or the convention.

Progressive

Half libertarian! The bedroom half.

You want the state out of your body and your bedroom, and in charge of the economy.

Conservative

Half libertarian! The wallet half.

You want the state out of your wallet and in charge of everyone's morals.

Statist

You'd license the lemonade stand, then tax the lemons.

You trust the state with your money and your choices. Bold.

Centrist

You're the human 'it depends.'

You landed in the middle on both money and personal freedom.

Collectivist in a Gadsden Flag

Liberty for me, checkpoints for thee.

You said liberty only works for people who share your heritage. That's not libertarian. That's tribal.

How you get sorted

First the quiz finds your spot on two axes, economic freedom and personal freedom. Land in the top right and it looks at your answers on anarchy, voting, tradition, the Fed, the Constitution and the rest to pick which flavor of libertarian you are. Land anywhere else and you're a progressive, conservative, statist or centrist. One answer overrides everything: say liberty only works for people who share your heritage, and you're a Collectivist in a Gadsden Flag no matter what else you said. Full scoring details.

Libertarian Enough is an independent libertarian organizing project. It is not a political party and is not affiliated with the Libertarian National Committee or any state party.

Made in Ohio by Travis Vought, author of The Asymmetric Libertarian. Every jab here is aimed from inside the tent.

Conservative

Half libertarian! The wallet half.

Who you are

You want the state out of your wallet and in charge of everyone's morals.

The roast

You hate big government right up until it's arresting someone you don't like.

Real-world kin: the fusionists liberty republicans on the factions page.

If you want to actually get something done

You're with us on taxes and guns. Now apply the same skepticism to the drug war and the surveillance state.

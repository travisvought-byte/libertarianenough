The quiz

Are you libertarian enough?

40 statements, five answers each, about five minutes. At the end you get your libertarian type, a purity score out of 100, your spot on the spectrum next to Rothbard, Marx and your HOA, and receipts for every purity trap.

  • No sign-up, no tracking of answers. Your answers stay in this browser. The results link holds them, so you only share them if you send it.
  • "Not sure" is a real answer. The purity police will notice, but it's allowed.
  • It's a joke with a real spectrum underneath. How scoring works.

Libertarian Enough is an independent libertarian organizing project. It is not a political party and is not affiliated with the Libertarian National Committee or any state party.

Made in Ohio by Travis Vought, author of The Asymmetric Libertarian. Every jab here is aimed from inside the tent.

What this is, and what it isn't

An organizing body, not a party

No ballot line, no national committee, no nine-hour bylaws debate. Libertarian Enough builds tools and playbooks for the people doing the work. It isn't affiliated with the Libertarian National Committee or any state party.

We don't take your money

No donations, no dues, no pass-through fund. State affiliates raise their own money in their own states, and we show them how. How the money works.

Facts first, sass second

Every fact on this site has a source and a date. The jokes are aimed at behavior and ideas, never at who people are. If we got something wrong, tell us.

Signs your local group is a debate club

  • Your last meeting ran three hours and ended with a motion to table the motion to table.
  • You have more people who can explain the Austrian business cycle than people who can name your county auditor.
  • Your candidate list for next year is "whoever shows up."
  • You lost a precinct seat that took 3 votes to win. Nobody filed.

If two of those landed, start with the organizing playbook. If all four did, start with the book.

Libertarian Enough is an independent libertarian organizing project. It is not a political party and is not affiliated with the Libertarian National Committee or any state party.

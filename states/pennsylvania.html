Cannabis

Adults 21 and older

Medical patients only No. Pennsylvania has no adult-use law. Without a medical card, possessing up to 30 grams of marijuana or 8 grams of hashish is a misdemeanor punishable by up to 30 days in jail, a fine of up to $500, or both. Law: Controlled Substance, Drug, Device and Cosmetic Act (Act 64 of 1972). Checked 2026-10-28. Source: legis.state.pa.us (opens in a new tab)

Medical

Allowed Yes. Medical use is lawful only for a registered patient or caregiver with a Department of Health ID card and practitioner certification. A dispensary may sell no more than a 90-day supply, and smoking medical marijuana is unlawful. Law: Medical Marijuana Act (Act 16 of 2016). Checked 2026-10-28. Source: legis.state.pa.us (opens in a new tab)

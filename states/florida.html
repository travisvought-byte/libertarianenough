Cannabis

Adults 21 and older

Medical patients only No. Florida has no adult-use law. Possessing 20 grams of cannabis or less is a first-degree misdemeanor; larger amounts are felonies. Law: Fla. Stat. 893.13(6). Checked 2026-11-15. Source: leg.state.fl.us (opens in a new tab)

Medical

Allowed Yes. Medical marijuana and low-THC cannabis are available to qualified patients. A qualified physician orders them, and licensed medical marijuana treatment centers are the only businesses allowed to dispense them. Law: Florida Office of Medical Marijuana Use. Checked 2026-11-15. Source: knowthefactsmmj.com (opens in a new tab)

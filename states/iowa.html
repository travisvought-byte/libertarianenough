Cannabis

Adults 21 and older

Not permitted No. Iowa has no adult-use law. A first conviction for possessing marijuana can bring up to six months in jail, a fine of up to $1,000, or both. Penalties rise for repeat offenses. Law: Iowa Code 124.401(5). Checked 2026-11-15. Source: legis.iowa.gov (opens in a new tab)

Medical

Limited Medical cannabidiol card program. The Iowa Department of Health and Human Services registers patients 18 and older who have a health care practitioner’s certification, with cards good for one year. Cardholders buy from five licensed dispensaries. The program page also links a waiver form for the limit of 4.5 grams of THC per 90 days. Law: Iowa Department of Health and Human Services, Medical cannabis patients and caregivers. Checked 2026-11-15. Source: hhs.iowa.gov (opens in a new tab)

Cannabis

Adults 21 and older

Allowed Yes, 21 and older. Only adults 21 and older can buy and possess cannabis. Law: Washington State Liquor and Cannabis Board, Know the law. Checked 2026-11-15. Source: lcb.wa.gov (opens in a new tab)

Medical

Allowed Yes, with a practitioner’s authorization. With a health care practitioner’s authorization, an adult patient may grow up to four plants. A recognition card from a medically endorsed store adds tax exemptions and higher limits: by default up to 3 ounces of usable cannabis (or equivalent products) and six plants. Patients 18 to 20 must register to buy. Law: Washington State Department of Health; RCW 69.51A.210. Checked 2026-11-15. Sources: doh.wa.gov (opens in a new tab), lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov (opens in a new tab)

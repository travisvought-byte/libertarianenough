Cannabis

Adults 21 and older

Allowed Yes, 21 and older. State law (AS 17.38) makes marijuana use legal only for people 21 and older. An adult may possess up to 1 ounce for personal use. Law: Alaska Alcohol & Marijuana Control Office, Marijuana FAQs (AS 17.38). Checked 2026-11-15. Source: commerce.alaska.gov (opens in a new tab)

Medical

Allowed Yes, registry cards. Medical cards are issued under AS 17.37. The adult-use law did not change their privileges or prohibitions. Law: Alaska Alcohol & Marijuana Control Office, Marijuana FAQs (AS 17.37). Checked 2026-11-15. Source: commerce.alaska.gov (opens in a new tab)

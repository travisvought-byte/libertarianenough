Michigan

The state affiliate

Libertarian Party of Michigan: michiganlp.org (opens in a new tab)

Listed on the Libertarian National Committee's state affiliates page.

Worth knowing: Two groups have claimed the Libertarian Party of Michigan name since 2022; in Libertarian National Committee v. Saliba a federal court barred the faction the LNC does not recognize from using the party's trademark, and the Sixth Circuit largely upheld that injunction.

Public information, listed the same way for every state. Not a partnership. Cannabis

Adults 21 and older

Allowed Yes, 21 and older. Michigan law allows personal possession and use of marijuana by people 21 and older. Law: Michigan Cannabis Regulatory Agency, Consumer connection. Checked 2026-11-15. Source: michigan.gov (opens in a new tab)

Medical

Allowed Yes. The Michigan Medical Marijuana Program registers patients and caregivers. Law: Michigan Cannabis Regulatory Agency, Consumer connection. Checked 2026-11-15. Source: michigan.gov (opens in a new tab)

Full state-by-state rules on possession, home grow, driving and more: The Reefer (opens in a new tab).

Sex work

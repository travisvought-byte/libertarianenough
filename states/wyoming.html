Cannabis

Adults 21 and older

Not permitted No. Wyoming has no adult-use law. Possessing up to 3 ounces of marijuana in plant form is a misdemeanor punishable by up to 12 months in jail, a $1,000 fine, or both. Larger amounts are a felony punishable by up to 5 years in prison, a $10,000 fine, or both. Law: W.S. 35-7-1031(c). Checked 2026-11-15. Source: Wyoming Legislature (opens in a new tab)

Medical

Not permitted No medical marijuana law. Wyoming law bars practitioners from prescribing or dispensing marijuana or THC except FDA-approved drugs such as dronabinol. Its former article on supervised medical use of hemp extracts (W.S. 35-7-1901 to 35-7-1903) was repealed in 2019, and hemp products are now outside the controlled substances law. Law: W.S. 35-7-1031(c) and 35-7-1063. Checked 2026-11-15. Source: Wyoming Legislature (opens in a new tab)

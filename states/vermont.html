Cannabis

Adults 21 and older

Allowed Yes, 21 and older. Adults 21 and older with a valid government-issued photo ID may buy cannabis. Law: Vermont Cannabis Control Board, Frequently asked questions. Checked 2026-11-15. Source: ccb.vermont.gov (opens in a new tab)

Medical

Allowed Yes. Vermont has a medical cannabis program. Law: Vermont Cannabis Control Board. Checked 2026-11-15. Source: ccb.vermont.gov (opens in a new tab)

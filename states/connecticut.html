Cannabis

Adults 21 and older

Allowed Yes, 21 and older. The state’s adult-use page says residents over 21 can legally possess and consume cannabis, and that possessing 1.5 ounces is legal. Law: Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection, Adult-use cannabis in Connecticut. Checked 2026-11-15. Source: portal.ct.gov (opens in a new tab)

Medical

Allowed Yes. Connecticut’s medical marijuana program continues to serve patients alongside adult use. Law: Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection, Adult-use cannabis in Connecticut. Checked 2026-11-15. Source: portal.ct.gov (opens in a new tab)

Full state-by-state rules on possession, home grow, driving and more: The Reefer (opens in a new tab).

Steal from the big parties

Libertarians love to say both parties are the same. On organizing, they're not the same as us: they're better. Steal everything that works and leave the rest.

What not to steal

  • Pay-to-play. A $15,000 sponsor table buys access. We'd rather it bought a petition drive.
  • Running everything from the top. Our advantage is that nobody's waiting for permission. Keep it that way.
  • Fundraising emails that scream. "FINAL NOTICE" for a $5 ask burns trust. Tell people what the money did.

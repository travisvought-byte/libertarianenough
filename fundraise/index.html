How the money works

We don't take donations

There's no donate button on this site and there won't be one until we've said exactly where the money goes. Right now, Libertarian Enough runs on volunteer time.

We don't hand out money

No national pot, no pass-through. Money raised in Ohio should be spent by Ohioans, and the same goes for every other state.

We give states the tools

Playbooks, templates, event kits and free websites, so each state affiliate can raise its own money, under its own state's rules, from its own people.

What's coming

  • Event kits: a dinner and a cheap cookout, with ticket ladders, run-of-show and sponsor sheets.
  • Donate pages built into the free websites, pointing at each group's own processor.
  • A state-by-state sheet of the basic fundraising rules, starting with the states that ask first.

The one-sentence version

Ask often, ask small, ask monthly, say exactly what the money will do, then show that it did it. Every party that raises real money does those five things. Most libertarian groups do none of them.

Libertarian Enough is an independent libertarian organizing project. It is not a political party and is not affiliated with the Libertarian National Committee or any state party.

Cause

Bodily autonomy

Self-ownership starts at your skin. What you put in your body, what treatment you try, and how you face the end belong to you.

Say it in 10 seconds: "Your body, your decisions, from the medicine you try to the care you choose at the end."

A control exposure message, in the language of the messaging playbook.

Where things stand

  • The federal Right to Try Act, signed May 30, 2018, lets patients with a life-threatening disease who have exhausted approved treatments and can't join a clinical trial seek an unapproved drug that has completed Phase 1 testing. Companies are not required to provide the drug, and the FDA does not review individual requests. As of 2018 law, current FDA guidance. Source: FDA (opens in a new tab)
  • Oregon (Measure 109, 2020) and Colorado (Proposition 122, 2022) legalized psilocybin access through voter initiatives. New Mexico became the third state, and the first through its legislature, when Gov. Michelle Lujan Grisham signed the Medical Psilocybin Act (SB 219) on April 7, 2025. Its program must be running by December 31, 2027. As of 2025-04. Source: foley.com (opens in a new tab)
  • Medical aid in dying is authorized in 13 states and D.C.: California, Colorado, Delaware, Hawaii, Illinois, Maine, Montana (by court ruling), New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, Oregon, Vermont, Washington and the District of Columbia. As of 2026. Source: compassionandchoices.org (opens in a new tab)
  • On March 29, 2023 the FDA approved Narcan 4 mg naloxone nasal spray for sale without a prescription, the first over-the-counter opioid overdose reversal drug. As of 2023-03-29. Source: FDA (opens in a new tab)
  • By April 2023, 36 states plus D.C. had legalized fentanyl test strips, which many states had treated as illegal drug paraphernalia. Fourteen states still classed them as illegal at that time. As of 2023-04. Source: smithsonianmag.com (opens in a new tab)
  • Libertarians split on abortion. Pro-choice libertarians say bodily autonomy means the state has no business in the decision. Pro-life libertarians, such as Libertarians for Life, say a human before birth has the same rights as one after birth, including the right to life. The Libertarian Party's platform said from 2012 to May 2022 that 'government should be kept out of the matter'. The party dropped that plank in 2022. As of 2022. Source: Wikipedia (opens in a new tab)

Do this locally

  1. Hold naloxone trainings and hand out kits at community events and meetings.
  2. Ask your city or county to fund or allow fentanyl test strip distribution, and testify where state paraphernalia law still gets in the way.
  3. Host a public panel on end-of-life options or psychedelic-therapy laws with doctors, patients and opponents.

Each one is a real-world win you can show voters. That's the whole Asymmetric Libertarian idea: pick one system, fix it, make it visible.

Who's already fighting

  • Compassion & Choices (opens in a new tab): National nonprofit campaigning for medical aid in dying and other end-of-life options; tracks state laws.
  • Drug Policy Alliance (opens in a new tab): National nonprofit working to reduce the harms of drug use and punitive drug laws, including naloxone, drug checking and syringe services.
  • National Harm Reduction Coalition (opens in a new tab): National network supporting harm reduction programs, including fentanyl resources and test strip work.
  • NEXT Distro (opens in a new tab): Online service that connects people with naloxone and drug-checking supplies through state partner programs.

Listing an organization isn't an endorsement or a partnership. It's who's doing the work.

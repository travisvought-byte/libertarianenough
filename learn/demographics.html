The numbers

Somewhere between 7% and 27% of Americans, depending on who asks and how. Here's every number and why they don't match.

Why the numbers disagree

  • Each survey defines 'libertarian' differently, so the numbers range from 7% to 27% and cannot be combined. Pew (2014) counts people who call themselves libertarian and can define the word. PRRI (2013) uses a multi-question Libertarian Orientation Scale. Gallup sorts people with just two questions (role of government and promoting values), which produces the largest numbers. Cato's Boaz, Kirby and Ekins count voters who give fiscally conservative, socially liberal answers. Ekins' Reason-Rupe figures are self-identification.
  • Pew found that many self-described libertarians do not hold consistently libertarian views, and many people with libertarian views don't use the label.
  • PRRI's demographic percentages describe only the 7% of respondents classified as consistent libertarians, a small subsample of 2,317 adults.
  • The newest high-quality national survey data on libertarian identity found here is from 2013 to 2016. Gallup's 2015 figures are reported through Reason because Gallup and Cato pages could not be loaded directly.
  • Party registration counts cover only states that register voters by party. Many states have no party registration, so the national figure undercounts supporters. Ballot Access News tables mix dates across states.
  • 'Dues-paying,' 'sustaining' and 'life' member counts are not always the same measure. The 2024 to 2026 figures come from Third Party Watch, an independent commentary site citing party reports, and should be treated as directional.
  • The 2013 PRRI and 2014 Pew studies both found a large gender gap, roughly two men for every woman among libertarians.

What it means

Lots of Americans hold libertarian-ish views, far fewer call themselves libertarian, and a tiny slice pay dues to the party. That gap is the whole opportunity. The people are out there. The organizing isn't. Fix that.

Libertarian Enough is an independent libertarian organizing project. It is not a political party and is not affiliated with the Libertarian National Committee or any state party.

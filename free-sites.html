Free websites

If you're a libertarian group or a candidate who needs a website, we'll build it free. You pay for your domain name, about the cost of a pizza a year, and nothing else.

What you get

  • A fast, simple site on your own domain, hosted free on GitHub Pages. No monthly hosting bill, no plugin updates, nothing to get hacked.
  • Accessible by default. Built to WCAG AA: readable contrast, keyboard friendly, screen-reader friendly, big tap targets.
  • The pages that matter: who you are, your causes, events, candidates, how to join, and a donate button that points to your processor and your account.
  • Privacy-friendly visitor counts with no cookies.
  • You own it. The code lives in a repository you can take anywhere.

Who qualifies

  • Libertarian state, county and campus groups, whatever their national affiliation.
  • Candidates running on libertarian ideas, in any party or none, especially for local and nonpartisan office.
  • Single-issue groups working on the causes on this site.

What we won't do

  • Handle your money. Donations go straight to your own account.
  • Write your platform or speak for you.
  • Build anything that targets people for who they are. Punch at ideas and behavior all you want.

What we've built

These are run by the same builder. They're examples of the kind of site you'd get, not endorsements by them.

  • Find Bingo (opens in a new tab): A national directory of bingo games.
  • Ohio Bingo (opens in a new tab): Every bingo game in Ohio.
  • The Reefer (opens in a new tab): Cannabis law, state by state.
  • OhioWeed.org (opens in a new tab): Ohio's cannabis guide.
  • Abuse of Office (opens in a new tab): Public records on officials, lawmakers and lobbyists.

How to ask

A request form is coming. For now, open a request on GitHub with your group's name, what you need, and a public way to confirm you're who you say you are.

Libertarian Enough is an independent libertarian organizing project. It is not a political party and is not affiliated with the Libertarian National Committee or any state party.
